Credits

Weapons

The weapons fighters carry are pictures made from free 3D models, all released into the public domain (CC0) by their makers: the two swords, the axe and the dagger from KayKit Adventurers by Kay Lousberg; the club, knife, scythe, cutlass, tentacle, spear, crystal, hammer and rifle by Quaternius; and the wrench by CreativeTrio. The last eleven were found on Poly Pizza. Thank you.

The Crab's pincer is made from Crab hand by Poly by Google, used under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 licence; it was turned into a picture and trimmed to the claw. The Clockmaker's hands, the yo-yo and the Jester's juggling balls were made for this game.

Fighter emblems

The emblem on every fighter is an icon from game-icons.net, made by Lorc, Delapouite, Skoll, sbed, John Colburn, Carl Olsen, Cathelineau and PriorBlue, and used under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 licence. The icons were recoloured and resized to fit the fighters. Thank you to the artists.
